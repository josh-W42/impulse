# M7 — Joints: Design + Lean Plan

**Date:** 2026-09-26 · **Status:** approved · **Parent:** `2026-09-24-impulse-engine-design.md`

## Design (approach A: joints in the sequential-impulse solver)

- Joints are velocity constraints solved in the same iterations as contacts, with
  warm starting (impulses persist on the joint) and Baumgarte position bias.
  Rejected: soft constraints (pair with the deferred soft step), XPBD (lineage).
- **Ball-socket:** anchors coincide; solved as one 3×3 block (effective-mass matrix
  `K = Σ m⁻¹·I − [r]ₓ I⁻¹ [r]ₓ`), better converged than three scalar rows.
  Optional **cone limit** on the swing between two body axes (twist unlimited):
  one-sided row along `normalize(axisA × axisB)` when the angle exceeds the limit.
- **Hinge:** ball-socket + two angular rows keeping the axes aligned. Optional
  **angle limits** `[min, max]`: signed angle between reference perpendiculars about
  the axis; one-sided rows (accumulated impulse clamped to one sign) when outside.
- **Distance:** anchors kept `length` apart (one row along the anchor-to-anchor line).
- Joints may use a static body as a world anchor. `collide_connected` (default false)
  filters contacts between joined bodies. Joints link sleep islands. Joints on
  removed bodies go inert.
- Demo: "Joints" scene — swinging capsule chain, hinged plank rope bridge with balls
  dropped on it, and a ragdoll (ball-socket + cone limits at shoulders/hips/neck,
  limited hinges at elbows/knees).

## Tasks (TDD; clippy ×2; commit each)

**J1. Data model + validation** — `JointDesc` (BallSocket / Hinge / Distance),
`World::add_joint → JointId`, validation errors, `clear` removes joints.
**J2. Solver rows** (ball-socket block, hinge angular rows, distance row, limits).
**J3. Contact filtering + islands.**
**J4. Tests:** pendulum period within 3% of `2π√(L/g)`; ball-socket anchors < 1 cm
apart while swinging; distance length within 1%; hinge rotates only about its axis;
hinge limit and cone limit hold (≤ limit + 0.05 rad); hanging 10-link chain stretches
< 2%; overlapping joined bodies stay calm; a resting chain sleeps as one island and
wakes as one; validation errors.
**J5. Joints demo scene.** **J6. Verify + ship; README refresh (final milestone).**

## Results

- 12 joint contract tests pass (`crates/physics/tests/joints.rs`), plus a scene
  test: every joint gap in the Joints scene stays within 3 cm of its start
  over 6 s.
- Tests proven non-vacuous by mutation:
  - With joint solving disabled, 9 of 12 fail. The other 3 are validation and
    removal tests, which don't depend on solving.
  - With the limit rows disabled, the hinge-limit and cone-limit tests fail.
  - With joints no longer linking islands, the sleep/wake test and the chain
    test fail. Without island linking, links fall asleep one at a time while
    still joined to awake neighbors.
- Speculative limit rows (active within 0.25 rad of a limit, allowed to close
  only the remaining gap) stop fast swings at the limit without overshoot and
  bounce-back.
- Joints are solved before contacts in each iteration, so contacts get the
  last word on penetration.
- Browser: Joints scene at 60 fps on WebGPU and WebGL2 (physics ≈ 0.1 ms/step).
- Not done (YAGNI): motors, breakable joints, twist limits on cones, soft (spring)
  joints, and position-level (NGS) joint correction.
