# M9b — Full PBR: Design + Lean Plan

**Date:** 2026-09-26 · **Status:** approved · **Parent:** `2026-09-24-impulse-engine-design.md`
**Follows:** `2026-09-26-m9a-assets.md` (models, base-colour textures)

## Design

**Shading.** Cook–Torrance, the same model as glTF viewers.
- Specular: GGX distribution, height-correlated Smith visibility, Schlick
  Fresnel.
- Diffuse: Lambert, scaled by (1 − F) · (1 − metallic).
- F0 = mix(0.04, base colour, metallic).

**Lighting** (linear HDR):
- **Sun:** direct light with the existing PCF shadow; intensity in real units.
- **Procedural sky** (zenith / horizon / ground gradient plus a sun disc):
  - **Diffuse:** projected at startup onto 9 SH coefficients by CPU numerical
    integration.
  - **Specular:** the sky sampled in the reflected direction, blurred toward
    its irradiance as roughness grows; environment BRDF from Karis' analytic
    approximation (no LUT).
- **Occlusion** applies to ambient light only; **emissive** is added last.
- **Output:** fixed exposure → Khronos PBR Neutral → the sRGB target.
- **Background:** the sky drawn by a fullscreen triangle through the same
  exposure and tonemap.
- **Built-in shapes** default to roughness 0.6, metallic 0.

**Assets crate:**
- Reads base colour, metallic-roughness, normal, occlusion and emissive
  textures, plus their factors (base colour, metallic, roughness, normal scale,
  occlusion strength, emissive).
- Colour spaces: base colour and emissive are sRGB; the rest are linear. Mips
  average the linear maps' raw values, and normal-map mips are renormalized.
- Tangents come from the file, or are generated from UVs (per-triangle, summed
  per vertex, with handedness). This is a simplified MikkTSpace — documented.
- Out of scope: the emissive-strength extension and alpha blending (all
  materials render opaque).

**Renderer:**
- Vertices gain a tangent (vec4).
- Material bind group (group 1): 5 textures, 1 sampler, and a factors uniform.
- Defaults are shared 1×1 textures: white, flat normal, black.
- Instance data gains roughness and metallic.
- M9a's baked base-colour factor is removed.
- Globals gain the sky parameters and the SH coefficients.

**Scenes:**
- `ObjectDesc` gains `roughness` and `metallic` (serde defaults 0.6 / 0).
- The inspector shows them for built-in shapes only; models use their file's
  material, times the colour tint.

**Demo.** A new **Materials** scene: a sphere chart (roughness across,
metallic down the rows) plus models.

**Test asset.** Khronos WaterBottle (CC0, 9 MB), imported via `models/` and not
committed. Download only after approval.
