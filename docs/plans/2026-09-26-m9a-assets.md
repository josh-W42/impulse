# M9a — Models (glTF): Design + Lean Plan

**Date:** 2026-09-26 · **Status:** approved · **Parent:** `2026-09-24-impulse-engine-design.md`
**Follow-up:** M9b — full PBR (metallic-roughness BRDF, normal/occlusion/emissive,
procedural-sky IBL, in-shader ACES tonemapping), designed when it starts.

## Design

**`impulse-assets` crate.** Depends on `gltf`, `image` (PNG/JPEG only) and glam;
no render or physics dependencies.
- Parses `.glb` into plain data: meshes (position, normal, UV, u32 indices),
  materials (base colour factor and texture), and RGBA8 textures with a mip
  chain.
- Node transforms are baked in. Skins and animations are ignored (rest pose).
- Only `.glb`, since external buffers can't be followed from a browser file
  picker.
- Limits (errors, never panics): file ≤ 32 MB, ≤ 500k vertices, textures ≤ 4096²,
  triangles only (other primitives are skipped; an error if nothing remains).

**Model registry** (name → parsed model):
- **Bundled library** — compiled in with `include_bytes!`, so loading is
  synchronous and identical on native and web. About 8 small CC0 models, plus
  one or two CC0 Khronos samples for M9b. Sources and licences go in
  `assets/models/LICENSES.md`.
- **Imported** — native: the `.glb` files in `models/`; browser: a file
  picker, lasting the session.

**Scene format.** A new shape
`Model(name: "barrel", scale: 1.0, collider: Hull)`.
- Colliders:
  - `Hull` (the default) — the convex hull of the mesh's extreme points in
    about 64 sampled directions;
  - `Box`, `Sphere`, `Capsule` — fitted to the mesh bounds.
- Scale is uniform. The format version stays at 1.
- A missing model gives `SceneError` "model 'x' isn't loaded; import it first",
  and the current scene is kept.

**Renderer:**
- Vertices gain `uv`, and indices become u32 everywhere.
- One texture and sampler bind group per material; built-in meshes use a 1×1
  white texture.
- Colour = texture × factor × instance colour (so tint and colour edits still
  apply).
- A model with several materials is several meshes sharing the body transform.
- GPU meshes and textures are uploaded once per model.

**Placement.**
- The body sits at the collider's centre (the hull's centre of mass, or the
  bounds centre for fitted colliders), and the render mesh is offset to match.
- Scale applies to both the mesh and the collider.
- Mass comes from the inspector; inertia from the collider.

**Editor:**
- **Add → Model…** picker.
- The inspector edits model, scale and collider type; a change rebuilds
  immediately.
- A **show colliders** wireframe toggle.
- Import: native **Models…** list, browser **Import…** picker; errors go to
  the panel.

**Demo.** A new **Props** scene: bundled models dropped into the pit.

**Deferred:**
- non-uniform scale;
- concave (triangle-mesh) colliders — models collide as convex shapes;
- animation and skins.

## Results

- **209 tests pass** across the workspace. New tests cover:
  - **`.glb` loading** (test files built in code): baked and nested transforms;
    mirrored nodes keep triangles facing out (fails without the winding fix);
    normals computed when missing; materials and textures come through;
    sRGB mips average in linear light; each bad input gives a specific error.
    Every bundled model also loads.
  - **Colliders:** extreme points are exactly a box's corners and match the
    model's reach in every sampled direction; fitted box, sphere and capsule
    enclose every vertex.
  - **Models in scenes:**
    - every bundled hull builds in physics at two scales;
    - meshes line up with their colliders (hull vertices lie on the placed
      mesh, and the box equals the placed mesh's bounds);
    - `Model` shapes round-trip through RON, and files may omit the collider;
    - a missing model gives a specific error naming it;
    - imports are named by file stem, and can't replace bundled models;
    - tinting happens in linear light;
    - new models come in at about 1 m and rest on the floor;
    - the Props scene settles inside the pit.
- **Bundle:** the wasm grew from 10.51 MB to 11.69 MB (+1.18 MB, +11%): 216 KB of
  models plus the PNG/JPEG decoders and glTF parsing. JS is unchanged at 147 KB.
- **Startup:** parsing the bundled library takes 29 ms (native release), once
  per process, cached.
- **Changes from the design:**
  - New and imported models are auto-scaled so their largest dimension is about
    1 m. The Kenney props are around 0.25 m; imports can be in any units.
  - A material's base-colour factor is baked into its texture (or a 1×1
    texture for untextured materials), so the shader needed no per-material
    uniform. M9b replaces this with real material parameters.
  - After review: on desktop, `.glb` files in `models/` appear directly in
    Add → Model… (picking one imports and adds it); a separate Import step
    wasn't discoverable. The browser keeps Import… (a file picker).
  - The renderer "pins" model uploads so the per-edit hull-mesh refresh
    doesn't re-upload them.
- **Not verified by automation:** the browser pane was hidden, so visuals on
  WebGPU and WebGL2, and browser import, are left to the manual check.
