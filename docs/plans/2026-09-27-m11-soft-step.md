# M11 — Soft Step Solver: Design + Lean Plan

**Date:** 2026-09-27 · **Status:** complete (pending ship) · **Parent:** `2026-09-24-impulse-engine-design.md`
**Follows:** `2026-09-26-m4-stability.md` (10-high tower: 4.6 cm drift, 0.066 m/s —
the known limit), `2026-09-26-m7-joints.md` (soft joint rows).

## Goal

Rebuild the step in the manner of Box2D v3's soft step: soft contacts,
substepping, a relax pass, and restitution in its own pass. The target is a
10-high tower that meets the 5-high bar (drift < 1 cm, final-second speed
< 0.01 m/s, tilt < 1°), with a 20-high tower standing as a stretch goal. The
classic solver stays, selectable, for live comparison.

## Design

**Per frame** (dt = 1/60):
1. Broadphase, narrowphase and waking, shared with classic.
2. Prepare soft contacts: body offsets, normal, effective masses, the initial
   separation (negative when overlapping), relative normal velocity (kept for
   restitution), and warm-start impulses from the cache.
3. **n substeps** (default 4, h = dt/n):
   - integrate velocities (gravity, mouse drag);
   - warm start;
   - solve with bias: joints (re-prepared from current poses) and contacts
     (normal, then friction);
   - integrate positions.
4. **Relax:** one bias-free solve pass.
5. **Restitution:** for contacts whose approach speed exceeded 1 m/s, with the
   target set from the pre-solve normal velocity.
6. Store impulses in the cache, then sleeping and broadphase sync as before.

**Soft contact normal:**
- The same spring-damper formulation as the M7 joints (bias rate, mass scale,
  impulse scale).
- Box2D v3 defaults (confirmed in `b2DefaultWorldDef`): 30 Hz, damping ratio
  10, push-out capped at 3 m/s, restitution threshold 1 m/s. The frequency is
  capped at a quarter of the substep rate.
- Current separation = initial separation + (pa − pb)·n, with the contact
  anchors tracked on their bodies, so there is no re-collision per substep.
- A positive separation is speculative (it may close that gap and no more).
- Friction is rigid, clamped by the normal impulse.

**Code:**
- New `solver_soft.rs`; `World::step` branches on
  `SolverSettings { mode: Classic | SoftStep, substeps }`. The classic path is
  untouched.
- Contact cache format shared; joint rows reused.
- Soft step is the default.
- Panel: a solver switch, with substeps in soft step and iterations in classic.
- Profiler phases unchanged.

## Baseline (classic solver, K1)

10 s, sleeping off (so resting motion can't hide), `tests/soft_step.rs`:

| Tower | Worst drift | Final-second speed | Worst tilt |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | 2.1 mm | 0.0002 m/s | 0.03° |
| 10 | 7.8 cm | 0.84 m/s | 0.76° |
| 20 | collapses (23 m) | 11.8 m/s | 175° |

The 10-tower is worse over 10 s than M4's shorter run suggested (0.066 m/s).

Rain bench (`cargo run --release -p impulse-physics --example bench`, native):
1,000 bodies, 1.26 ms mean, 3.45 ms p95, 3.86 ms max.

## Results

Towers, 10 s, sleeping off (`report_towers` in `tests/soft_step.rs`):

| Tower | Classic drift / late speed / tilt | Soft step drift / late speed / tilt |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | 2.1 mm / 0.0002 m/s / 0.03° | 2.1 mm / 0.00004 m/s / 0° |
| 10 | 7.8 cm / 0.84 m/s / 0.76° | **5.5 mm / 0.0023 m/s / 0°** |
| 20 | collapses (23 m) | collapses (7.4 m) |

**Goal met:** the soft 10-tower passes the 5-tower bar (a contract test).
**Stretch missed:** the 20-tower buckles. Soft contacts in series sag in
proportion to their load (steady overlap ≈ λ/(m·h·ω²), independent of
damping), so the bottom of a tall stack is compressed most. 60 Hz contacts
with 8 substeps stood it (1.2 cm, 0.007 m/s), but 6 substeps collapsed:
marginal, so the Box2D defaults (30 Hz, 4 substeps) stay.

Rain bench (native, 1,000 bodies): classic 1.27 / 3.46 ms mean / p95, soft step
2.43 / 4.64 ms (`BENCHMARKS.md`).

Findings:
- **Relax every substep**, not once at the end (the first attempt did the latter
  and the 20-tower rocked more). Joints relax too, rigid with no bias; limits
  keep only their speculative part.
- **No slop.** The spring's steady overlap keeps resting contacts alive; a 5 mm
  dead zone let tall stacks rock.
- **Fixed lever arms** (from prepare, as Box2D does). Arms rotating with the
  body misplaced friction on a rolling ball: a sliding ball ended at 2.51 m/s
  instead of 3.57.
- Alternating the sweep direction per substep made the 5-tower worse; reverted.
- Per-mode test bars, documented at each test: pile depth 0.2 m (soft: the
  impact overlap is pushed out gradually, ≤ 3 m/s) and glancing angular
  momentum 1e-2 (soft: no slop, so the spring moves resting bodies slightly).
- Mutation check: removing the contact relax fails `pile_of_boxes_never_deeply_interpenetrates`.
- CI runs the physics suites again under classic (`--features classic-default`).
- Deviation: mouse drag still applies once per frame, not per substep; the total
  impulse is the same.
