# M11 — Soft Step Solver: Design + Lean Plan

**Date:** 2026-09-27 · **Status:** approved (design) · **Parent:** `2026-09-24-impulse-engine-design.md`
**Follows:** `2026-09-26-m4-stability.md` (10-high tower: 4.6 cm drift, 0.066 m/s —
the known limit), `2026-09-26-m7-joints.md` (soft joint rows).

## Goal

Rebuild the step in the manner of Box2D v3's soft step: soft contacts,
substepping, a relax pass, and restitution in its own pass. The target is a
10-high tower that meets the 5-high bar (drift < 1 cm, final-second speed
< 0.01 m/s, tilt < 1°), with a 20-high tower standing as a stretch goal. The
classic solver stays, selectable, for live comparison.

## Design

**Per frame** (dt = 1/60):
1. Broadphase, narrowphase and waking, shared with classic.
2. Prepare soft contacts: body offsets, normal, effective masses, the initial
   separation (negative when overlapping), relative normal velocity (kept for
   restitution), and warm-start impulses from the cache.
3. **n substeps** (default 4, h = dt/n):
   - integrate velocities (gravity, mouse drag);
   - warm start;
   - solve with bias: joints (re-prepared from current poses) and contacts
     (normal, then friction);
   - integrate positions.
4. **Relax:** one bias-free solve pass.
5. **Restitution:** for contacts whose approach speed exceeded 1 m/s, with the
   target set from the pre-solve normal velocity.
6. Store impulses in the cache, then sleeping and broadphase sync as before.

**Soft contact normal:**
- The same spring-damper formulation as the M7 joints (bias rate, mass scale,
  impulse scale).
- Box2D v3 defaults (confirmed in `b2DefaultWorldDef`): 30 Hz, damping ratio
  10, push-out capped at 3 m/s, restitution threshold 1 m/s. The frequency is
  capped at a quarter of the substep rate.
- Current separation = initial separation + (pa − pb)·n, with the contact
  anchors tracked on their bodies, so there is no re-collision per substep.
- A positive separation is speculative (it may close that gap and no more).
- Friction is rigid, clamped by the normal impulse.

**Code:**
- New `solver_soft.rs`; `World::step` branches on
  `SolverSettings { mode: Classic | SoftStep, substeps }`. The classic path is
  untouched.
- Contact cache format shared; joint rows reused.
- Soft step is the default.
- Panel: a solver switch, with substeps in soft step and iterations in classic.
- Profiler phases unchanged.
